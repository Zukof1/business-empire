# Dealership as a business, and a Lifestyle tab: design

Approved in chat on 2026-11-27. Rental Properties and App Studio follow separately.

## Car Dealership
Moves from its own tab into the Vault dashboard. The $2,500 license is its opening cost; anyone who has it keeps it.
Auctions, garage, demand and photos work as before. The alert dot moves to the Vault button and also covers broken taxis.

## Lifestyle tab
Takes the Dealer tab's place. Purchases earn nothing; they appear in a "Your life" collection with a lifestyle level.

| Type | Items and prices |
|---|---|
| Style | Designer wardrobe $5k, Luxury watch $25k, Diamond watch $250k |
| Homes | City apartment $100k, Beach house $750k, Mansion $5M |
| Toys | Sports car $50k, Yacht $2M, Private jet $50M |
| Experiences (repeatable) | Weekend getaway $10k, Huge party $100k, World tour $1M |

Levels by total spent: Getting by, Comfortable ($10k), Well off ($100k), Luxury living ($1M), High society ($10M),
Living legend ($50M).

## Builder's calls
- Each purchase asks for confirmation, since there are no refunds.
- The collection is kept through prestige.
- The Trade screen is titled "Stocks", so it is not confused with the new marketplace.
- Items use drawn icons until photos exist.
